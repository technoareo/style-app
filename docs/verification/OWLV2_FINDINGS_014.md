`owlv2-wasm-probe-014.mjs/.json` uses the existing local Transformers.js 4.3.1 / ONNX WASM single-thread runtime hook, explicit local-file-only model loading and no network inference. Generic clothing noun queries and threshold .2 were fixed for both development photos; there is no independent human-gold set. Input is explicitly black-padded to square before the processor resizes, preserving aspect ratio; normalized padded coordinates are converted to original display coordinates **without clipping**. No numeric preprocessing parity with the original Python model was established.

- Load: 1.289 s; total two-image experiment: 76.449 s.
- Single-shirt control: 37.236 s, four proposals, all inside original-image bounds. Two high-score proposals have **identical boxes** with aliases `shirt` and `t-shirt`; the other two are lower-score subregions. Four proposals are not four garments.
- Crowded closet: 37.475 s, nine proposals, **five unique box geometries**. One region is emitted under four alternative classes (`coat`, `trousers`, `dress`, `jacket`); another under two. Eight proposals fail the harness's strict original-frame bounds, with small border overhangs (minimum x about -.003, maximum y about 1.022), rather than reversed boxes. Such overhang can be ordinary detector extrapolation or padding/frame-conversion behavior; it does not alone prove a nonsensical model result. The only strictly in-bounds proposal is a small lower-right `trousers` region. Several unique boxes span broad portions of the rack rather than establish one correctly isolated garment each.

This produces more spatial candidates than the single full-rack Qwen result, but it does **not** establish useful whole-wardrobe coverage or accuracy. Do not count nine garments, silently repair boxes, use alias duplicates as recall, or integrate it on this evidence alone. A future provider adapter may explicitly define clipping/alias handling, preserving raw outputs and reporting transformations; that requires separate tests/review and independent gold evaluation. It must not conceal reversed/invalid geometry or suppress genuinely layered garments merely because their boxes overlap.
